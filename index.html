    Card Vault photos is for TCDB. Card Vault search is for SportsCardsPro, eBay (sale photos) and 130point (prices): it is the same as the one in the price help.
    Photos come from the TCDB checklists, then SportsCardsPro's catalog pictures, then the photo of the card's last sale, or from you (Edit → photo).
  2. Click From TCDB. A TCDB page opens (pass its security check if one appears). Click the Card Vault photos bookmark there and keep the tab open until it says done.
  3. The photos go straight to Card Vault and the cards update by themselves.
  4. Cards TCDB has no photo for: click SportsCardsPro. A SportsCardsPro page opens: click your Card Vault search bookmark there (the one from the price help) and keep the tab open until it says done. Only SportsCardsPro's own picture of exactly that card and version is taken.
  5. Cards neither TCDB nor SportsCardsPro has a photo of: click Sale photos. An eBay page opens: click Card Vault search there. It opens each card's saved sale and takes its photo (cards with no saved sale search 130point instead). A TCDB or SportsCardsPro photo replaces it when one turns up. Wrong one? Open the card → Wrong photo, or add yours with Edit.
?
  1. One time only: drag this button to your Chrome bookmarks bar → Card Vault search (if you have an older "Card Vault prices" bookmark, delete it; keep "Card Vault photos", it is for TCDB).
  2. Click SportsCardsPro. A SportsCardsPro page opens: click the Card Vault search bookmark there and keep the tab open until it says done. Each card is linked once to its own SportsCardsPro page (an exact match is linked by itself; when several pages could be your card, they wait in To check for you to pick one), and its ungraded price is read from that page every time. Wrong page? Open the card and click Wrong SportsCardsPro page.
  3. Cards SportsCardsPro does not have (or with no ungraded price there) go to 130point: click it, then the same bookmark on the 130point page. Each card is searched by its name and set; when that finds fewer than 3 sales, shorter searches (player + number, player + set) run too. The price is the middle of the last 3 sales of exactly your version of the card (same base, parallel or numbered version, ungraded, no autos unless yours is one); with only 1 sale it is marked uncertain; with no sale of that version, no price. A card with no price lists the sales the search saw and why each was not counted: This is my card takes one as its price.
  4. If a run stops before the end (130point not answering, or its daily limit), click 130point again later: it continues with the cards that were not reached.
  5. Prices you typed yourself are also updated when 130point finds sales of the card; if it finds none, your price stays. Cards updated in the last 7 days are skipped.
